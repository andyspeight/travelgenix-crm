1 Getting started

Luna Work is your travel-native CRM. Luna, the built-in AI, reads your inbox, briefs you on every customer, suggests the next move and runs the routine follow-ups. You stay in control: Luna drafts and queues, you approve and send.

Signing in & your workspace

  • Open your Luna Work URL. You land on the Dashboard.
  • The left sidebar is your map: Dashboard, Inbox, Customers, Trips, Journeys, Tasks, Reports, and Settings at the bottom.
  • Toggle light / dark mode with the sun/moon button in the bottom-left.

Getting data in

  1. Just exploring? Open Customers and click Seed demo data to load 30 realistic sample households so every screen has something to show.
  2. Going live? Add your own customers instead, and clear the demo data once you're ready for real records.

The guided tour

The first time you open Luna Work, a short step-by-step tour walks you through set-up and every screen. You can reopen it any time from Take a tour at the bottom of the sidebar.

Two ways to move fast

Quick find
  • Press Ctrl / ⌘ + P, or click Quick find at the top of the sidebar.
  • Jump straight to any customer, trip or page.
Ask Luna
  • The glowing button bottom-right (Ctrl / ⌘ + K).
  • Ask in plain English: "Who's travelling next month?", "How much have we booked this year?"
Tip: Ask Luna understands your real data. Try "Who's going to Spain?" or "Show me high-value customers who've gone quiet" and click straight through to the records.

2 Dashboard

Your home screen and daily starting point. It answers one question: what needs me today?

What you'll see

  • Morning briefing — a single sentence summarising messages that need you, imminent departures and live enquiry value.
  • KPIs — customers and lifetime value, live pipeline, departures in the next 7 days, and what needs you today. Each tile is clickable.
  • Pipeline by stage — value sitting in each stage of the trip pipeline.
  • Departures & travelling — who's away now and who leaves soon.
  • Needs you today — the urgent inbox items, straight from Luna's triage.
  • Luna on auto-pilot — what your journeys did in the background.

How to use it

  1. Read the briefing line first — it's your to-do list in one sentence.
  2. Click Needs you today or any KPI tile to jump straight to the work.
  3. Glance at the auto-pilot strip to see what Luna handled while you were away.
Tip: Make the Dashboard your first stop each morning. If the briefing says "nothing urgent", that's your cue to chase quotes or re-engage past travellers.

3 Customers & smart segments

Your customer list, with a search box that understands plain English. Describe who you want and Luna builds the filter.

Finding the right people

  1. Type a description in the smart bar, e.g. "families who went to Greece and have gone quiet", and press Enter.
  2. Luna turns it into filter chips. Remove a chip to widen the search, or click Clear to reset.
  3. Use the saved segment chips for one-click common views (travelling now, high-value couples, and any you save).
  4. Use the plain text box to filter the visible list by name or city.

Acting on a group

  • Email all — opens a pre-addressed email draft to everyone in the current view.
  • Add to journey — enrol the group into an auto-pilot journey.
  • Save segment — name the search and pin it as a reusable chip.
  • Select rows (the checkboxes) to act on a specific subset: Email selected, Add tag, Draft outreach.

How to use it

  1. Search, refine with chips, then either click a customer to open them or act on the whole group.
  2. Found a view you'll want again? Save segment so it's one click next time.
Tip: Segments are the engine room of proactive selling. Save "Booked 18+ months ago, no contact" and work it once a week — that's repeat business most agencies leave on the table.
Set-up note: Saving segments needs the segments table enabled in your database (a one-time migration). Until then everything else works and Save segment will tell you what to run.

4 Customer 360

Everything about one household on a single page — history, intelligence and the next move.

What's on the page

  • Luna's brief — a short, plain-English summary of who they are and how they book.
  • Predictions — rebooking opportunity, a trip-match idea, and a risk read (passports, compliance). The numbers are computed from real data, not guessed.
  • Timeline — every email, chat, call and note in one thread.
  • Luna · Next steps — the best actions right now, each with a Do button.
  • Trips, household, preferences and compliance panels down the right.

Actions you can take

ButtonWhat it does
Refresh briefRegenerates Luna's brief and predictions from the latest data.
Draft a replyOpens the inbox focused on their most recent message, ready to reply.
Add noteAdds an internal note that appears in the timeline.
Schedule callCreates a follow-up task (and opens your booking page if configured).
Next steps · DoPerforms the suggested step — creates the task or opens the reply.

How to use it

  1. Open a customer, read the brief and the next steps — that's your 10-second catch-up.
  2. Work the next steps top-down with the Do buttons.
  3. After a call or change, click Add note so the next person sees the full picture.
Tip: Before any call, open the record and skim the brief and timeline. Mentioning their last trip or a child's name by memory is what turns a booking into a relationship.

5 Inbox

Every inbound message, triaged by Luna and ready to answer — with reply options written for you.

Triage lanes

  • Luna sorts messages into Today, This week, Later and All, with a one-line reason for each.
  • Work the Today lane first — those are the ones that move money or matter most.

Replying

  1. Select a message. The middle pane shows the full email and Luna's read of it.
  2. Below it sit ranked reply drafts, each with a one-line rationale for the trade-off it makes.
  3. Switch between drafts, then Send as is, Edit before sending, or click Draft with Luna to generate fresh options grounded in that customer's record.
  4. The right pane is a mini customer card — context without leaving the inbox.
Tip: The drafts are deliberately different in tone, not three versions of the same thing. Pick the one that fits the relationship — the rationale tells you what each one prioritises.

6 Trips pipeline

A Kanban board of every trip, from first enquiry to safely returned, so nothing stalls.

The stages

Enquiry → Quoted → Booked → Pre-departure → Travelling → Returned. Cancelled trips are kept out of the pipeline but reachable via the toggle.

How to use it

  1. Drag a card between columns as a trip moves forward. On touch or keyboard, use the stage dropdown on each card.
  2. Read the column totals to see how much value is sitting at each stage.
  3. Click a card to open the customer behind the trip.
Tip: A healthy board has movement every day. If cards pile up in Quoted, that's your follow-up list — pair it with a journey so Luna chases them automatically.

7 Journeys (auto-pilot)

The heart of "AI doing the work nobody has time for". A journey is a rule: when something happens, Luna does a follow-up.

Getting started

  1. Open Journeys and click Install starter journeys to add four proven rules.
  2. Each card shows its trigger, its action, and how many customers are eligible right now.
  3. Click Run now on a journey (or Run all active) to act on the eligible customers.
  4. Pause any journey you don't want running.

The starter journeys

JourneyWhen it firesWhat Luna does
Pre-departure check-in10 days before departureCreates a task to confirm details & passports
Welcome home3 days after returnDrafts a warm welcome-home email
Passport expiry warningPassport within 6 months of expiryCreates a task to flag it
Re-engage quiet customersNo contact for 12 monthsDrafts a re-engagement email

The What Luna has done feed logs every action. Drafts are marked awaiting review — they never send on their own.

Tip: Turn journeys on one at a time and watch the activity feed for a few days. Once you trust a journey, leave it running and let it quietly protect your repeat revenue.
Good to know: Running a journey twice never doubles up — Luna remembers who it has already actioned.

8 Tasks

Your single work queue. Everything Luna queues from journeys — plus follow-ups you create — lands here.

How to use it

  1. Open tasks are bucketed by due date: Overdue, Today, This week, Later and No date.
  2. Tick the checkbox to complete a task, or Snooze it (it returns in three days).
  3. Switch the filter to Snoozed, Done or All. Reopen anything you need back.
  4. Tasks created by a journey carry a badge and link straight to the customer.
Tip: Clear Overdue and Today every morning and the rest of the CRM stays honest. The Dashboard's "needs you today" count includes these.

9 Reports

The numbers that matter, computed live from your trips and customers.

What's covered

  • Headline figures — booked revenue, open pipeline, bookings and average booking value.
  • Pipeline & conversion — where value sits and how it flows through the stages.
  • Revenue by destination and source attribution — what sells and where it comes from.
  • Business health — revenue trend, new vs repeat, customer value bands and lead time.

How to use it

  1. Flip the range between This year, Last 12 months and All time — it updates instantly.
  2. Use source attribution to decide where your next marketing pound goes.
Tip: Watch new-vs-repeat over time. Growing repeat share is the clearest sign your journeys and follow-ups are working.

10 Settings

Your workspace, your team, and how Luna behaves.

  • Workspace — agency name, brand colour, region and plan.
  • Team — the people with access and their roles.
  • Luna AI — the house style Luna always writes in (UK English, warm, direct, no filler) and which feature each model powers.
  • Integrations — connection status for your database, the AI engine and hosting.
  • Compliance & data — live GDPR consent, marketing opt-in and passport-expiry figures across your customers.
Tip: Check the Compliance panel before any marketing send — it shows at a glance how many customers have consented.

11 Luna AI & how to trust it

Luna is built to be helpful and safe. Here's exactly what's automated and what always waits for you.

The rules Luna follows

  • It never invents facts. Briefs, drafts and suggestions use only the data on file. If Luna doesn't know something, it leaves it out rather than guessing.
  • Numbers are computed, not guessed. Risk and opportunity scores come from real arithmetic on real fields, so you can rely on them.
  • Nothing sends itself. Luna drafts and queues; a human reviews and sends. Journey emails sit as drafts marked "awaiting review".
  • It writes in your voice. UK English, warm and direct, no marketing filler.

What needs to be switched on

  • Live AI (briefs, reply drafts, Ask Luna, smart segments) needs an AI key configured on your deployment. Without it, the app still runs and falls back to prepared content.
  • The Settings → Integrations panel shows you exactly what's connected.
Tip: Treat Luna like a brilliant new colleague: let it do the first draft and the legwork, and bring your judgement to the final send. That combination is where the time savings come from.

A simple daily rhythm

  1. Open the Dashboard and read the briefing.
  2. Clear the Today inbox lane and any overdue Tasks.
  3. Glance at Trips for anything stalled in Quoted.
  4. Once a week, work a saved segment of quiet past customers.
  5. Let Journeys handle the routine in the background.
