Where to start — by role

Luna Work suits the whole agency. Find yourself below for the fastest path to value. Page references point to the numbered sections.

Owner / Manager

You care about revenue, the team's output and nothing slipping through.
  • Start each day on the Dashboard (§2) — the briefing line is your pulse.
  • Set up Journeys (§7) once — they protect repeat revenue on auto-pilot.
  • Check Reports (§9) weekly; watch the new-vs-repeat trend.
  • Use Settings (§10) for team and compliance before any campaign.

Travel consultant / Agent

You live in the inbox and on the phone, turning enquiries into trips.
  • Clear the Inbox Today lane (§5) — reply with the ranked drafts.
  • Open the Customer 360 (§4) before every call; read the brief and next steps.
  • Keep the Trips board (§6) moving; chase anything stuck in Quoted.
  • Work your Tasks (§8) — overdue and today — to zero each morning.
  • Once a week, run a saved segment (§3) of quiet past customers.

Administrator / Operations

You keep the workspace healthy, compliant and set up correctly.
  • Own the set-up checklist (§1) — data, the optional AI key, the segments migration.
  • Monitor Settings → Compliance (§10) — consent, marketing opt-in, passports.
  • Keep Journeys (§7) tidy: pause what's not needed, review the activity feed.
  • Use Reports (§9) to pull figures for management.
New starter? Whatever your role, take the in-app tour first (sidebar → Take a tour), then read §1 and your role box. You'll be productive inside ten minutes.

1 Getting started

Luna Work is your travel-native CRM. Luna, the built-in AI, reads your inbox, briefs you on every customer, suggests the next move and runs the routine follow-ups. You stay in control: Luna drafts and queues, you approve and send.

Finding your way around

  • The left sidebar is your map: Dashboard, Inbox, Customers, Trips, Journeys, Tasks, Reports, with Take a tour and Settings at the bottom.
  • Each page opens with a top bar showing the page title and the main action for that screen.
  • Light / dark mode: the sun/moon button at the bottom-left of the sidebar. Your choice is remembered on that device.
  • On a phone or tablet: the sidebar collapses to a menu button (top-left). Tap it to open the navigation drawer.

Getting data in

  1. Just exploring? Open Customers and click Seed demo data to load 30 realistic sample households so every screen has something to show.
  2. Going live? Add your own customers instead, and clear the demo data once you're ready for real records (your admin can do this).

The guided tour & finding things fast

Take a tour
  • Auto-opens on first visit; reopen any time from the sidebar.
  • Walks you through set-up and every screen with deep links.
Quick find & Ask Luna
  • ⌘/Ctrl + P — jump to any customer, trip or page.
  • ⌘/Ctrl + K — ask Luna a plain-English question.
Good to knowQuick find vs Ask Luna. Quick find is a navigator — it jumps you to a record or page by name. Ask Luna is an analyst — it answers questions about your data (“who's travelling next month?”) and shows clickable results. Use Quick find to go somewhere, Ask Luna to find something out.

2 Dashboard

Your home screen and daily starting point. It answers one question: what needs me today?

How to use it

  1. Read the briefing line first — your to-do list in one sentence.
  2. Click Needs you today or any KPI tile to jump straight to the work.
  3. Glance at the Luna on auto-pilot strip to see what journeys handled overnight.
Good to knowEvery tile is a live figure, recalculated each time you open the page, and every tile is clickable — it takes you to the matching screen already filtered. The auto-pilot strip only appears once your journeys have done something, so an empty strip simply means nothing has run yet.
Top tip: If the briefing says “nothing urgent”, that's your cue to be proactive — chase a quote or work a re-engagement segment.

3 Customers & smart segments

Your customer list, with a search box that understands plain English. Describe who you want and Luna builds the filter.

Finding & acting on people

  1. Type a description in the smart bar and press Enter — Luna turns it into filter chips.
  2. Remove a chip to widen the search; use the saved-segment chips for one-click views.
  3. Act on the group: Email all, Add to journey, Save segment, or tick rows for Email selected / Add tag / Draft outreach.
  4. Click any customer to open their full record.

What you can filter on

You can ask for…Try phrasing it like
Destination they've travelled to“been to Greece”, “Italy lovers”, “Maldives”
Household type“families”, “couples”, “solo travellers”; “with kids” → families
Value (lifetime spend)“over £20k”, “high value”, “VIPs”, “big spenders”
Recency / dormancy“gone quiet”, “no contact in 12 months”, “booked in the last 6 months”, “booked in 2024”
Trip status“travelling now”, “pre-departure”, “at enquiry stage”, “recently returned”
Home town“in Bournemouth”, “Poole”, “Salisbury”
Good to knowChips combine with AND. Each chip narrows the result, so more chips means fewer (more precise) customers — if you get no results, remove a chip. The small text box above the table does a quick name/city filter on the rows already loaded; the smart bar is what actually queries your whole database. Email all / Add to journey act on the whole filtered view, unless you tick specific rows first — then they act only on your selection.
Top tip: Save “Booked 18+ months ago, no contact” and work it weekly — that's repeat business most agencies leave on the table.
Set-up note: Saving segments needs the one-time segments migration. Until then, everything else works and Save segment tells you exactly what to run.

4 Customer 360

Everything about one household on a single page — history, intelligence and the next move.

What's on the page

  • Brief — a short, plain-English summary of who they are and how they book.
  • Predictions — three cards (explained below).
  • Timeline — every email, chat, call and note in one thread, newest first.
  • Next steps — the best actions right now, each with a Do button.
  • Trips, household, preferences and compliance panels down the right.

What the three predictions mean

CardWhat it tells youHow it's worked out
OpportunityHow likely they are to book again soonFrom their booking cadence and recency. Needs 3+ past bookings; below that it says “insufficient history”.
Trip matchA destination idea that fits themFrom their trip history, tags and recorded preferences.
RiskAnything to handle before bookingFrom passport validity vs planned travel, GDPR consent and any contact flags. Calculated, never guessed.

Actions you can take

ButtonWhat it does
Refresh briefRegenerates Luna's brief and predictions from the latest data.
Draft a replyOpens the inbox focused on their most recent message.
Add noteAdds an internal note that appears in the timeline.
Schedule callCreates a follow-up task (and opens your booking page if configured).
Next steps · DoPerforms the suggested step — creates the task or opens the reply.
Good to knowThe household graph shows the whole family with ages and any allergy flags, so you never miss a child's nut allergy on a booking. The compliance panel is worked out live from the contacts (GDPR consent, marketing opt-in, passport validity) — there's nothing to maintain. Preferences can be edited as you learn them, and they feed the trip-match prediction. Notes you add appear in the timeline immediately.
Top tip: Skim the brief and timeline before every call. Mentioning their last trip or a child's name from memory turns a booking into a relationship.

5 Inbox

Every inbound message, triaged by Luna and ready to answer — with reply options written for you.

How to use it

  1. Work the Today lane first. Select a message to see the full email and Luna's read.
  2. Switch between the ranked drafts — each has a one-line rationale for its trade-off.
  3. Send as is, Edit before sending, or Draft with Luna for fresh, grounded options.
Good to knowWhat the lanes mean. Luna sorts each message by urgency: Today = needs a same-day reply (a worry, a time-sensitive enquiry); This week = important but not urgent; Later = nice-to-have. The percentage on each draft is Luna's confidence that draft fits this customer and message — highest first. The three-pane layout (list · message · customer card) stacks into a single column on phones.
Top tip: The drafts differ in tone on purpose. Pick the one whose rationale matches the relationship, not just the fastest one.

6 Trips pipeline

A Kanban board of every trip, from first enquiry to safely returned, so nothing stalls.

What each stage means

StageThe trip is…
EnquiryA new request, not yet quoted.
QuotedPriced and sent to the customer; awaiting a decision.
BookedConfirmed and paid (or deposit taken).
Pre-departureBooked and departing soon — final-details territory.
TravellingThe customer is away now.
ReturnedHome — time for a welcome-home and feedback.
CancelledAn exit, kept off the board but reachable via the toggle.

How to use it

  1. Drag a card between columns as a trip moves on (or use the stage dropdown on touch/keyboard).
  2. Read the column totals to see value sitting at each stage.
  3. Click a card to open the customer behind the trip.
Good to knowMoving a card saves instantly. If a save fails (e.g. you briefly lose connection) the card snaps back to where it was and shows a message, so the board is always honest — just try the move again.
Top tip: If cards pile up in Quoted, that's your hottest follow-up list — pair it with a journey so Luna chases them automatically.

7 Journeys (auto-pilot)

The heart of “AI doing the work nobody has time for”. A journey is a rule: when something happens, Luna does a follow-up.

How to use it

  1. Click Install starter journeys to add the four proven rules.
  2. Use Run now on a journey (or Run all active) to act on who's eligible; Pause any you don't want.
  3. Review the What Luna has done feed — drafts are marked awaiting review and never send themselves.
  4. You can also enrol a hand-picked group from a segment (Customers → Add to journey).
Starter journeyFiresLuna does
Pre-departure check-in10 days beforeTask: confirm details
Welcome home3 days after returnDraft welcome email
Passport expiry warning< 6 months to expiryTask: flag passport
Re-engage quiet customers12 months no contactDraft re-engagement
Good to know“Eligible now” is how many customers currently match the rule. Run acts on them and logs each one. Running twice never doubles up — Luna remembers who it has already actioned, so a customer never gets the same task or draft twice. A paused journey isn't evaluated at all. Email actions are always created as drafts for review, never sent automatically — so auto-pilot is safe to leave on.
Top tip: Turn journeys on one at a time and watch the feed for a few days. Once you trust one, leave it running and let it protect your repeat revenue.

8 Tasks

Your single work queue. Everything Luna queues from journeys — plus follow-ups you create — lands here.

How to use it

  1. Tick the checkbox to complete a task, or Snooze it (it returns in three days).
  2. Filter by Open / Snoozed / Done / All; reopen anything you need back.
  3. Journey-created tasks carry a badge and link straight to the customer.
Good to knowOpen tasks are grouped by due date — Overdue, Today, This week, Later and No date — so the most pressing work is always on top. Tasks come from three places: journeys, the customer-360 buttons (Schedule call, next-step Do), and anything you add yourself. The Dashboard's “needs you today” figure includes your open tasks.
Top tip: Clear Overdue and Today every morning and the whole CRM stays honest.

9 Reports

The numbers that matter, computed live from your trips and customers.

How to use it

  1. Flip the range between This year / Last 12 months / All time — it updates instantly.
  2. Use source attribution to decide where your next marketing pound goes.
Good to knowFigures are gross booking value. A trip counts as “booked” revenue once it reaches Booked, Pre-departure, Travelling or Returned; Enquiry and Quoted count as open pipeline. There's deliberately no margin/commission report yet, because commission isn't captured in the data — an honest revenue view beats a column of estimates.
Top tip: Watch new-vs-repeat over time. A growing repeat share is the clearest sign your journeys and follow-ups are working.

11 Luna AI & Ask Luna

Luna is built to be helpful and safe. Here's exactly what it does, what it won't do, and how to get great answers from it.

The four rules Luna always follows

  • It never invents facts. Briefs and drafts use only the data on file; if Luna doesn't know something, it leaves it out rather than guessing.
  • Numbers are computed, not guessed. Risk and opportunity come from real arithmetic on real fields.
  • Nothing sends itself. Luna drafts and queues; a human reviews and sends.
  • It writes in your voice. UK English, warm and direct, no marketing filler.

Asking Luna a question

Open Ask Luna (the button bottom-right, or ⌘/Ctrl + K) and type a question in normal English. Luna finds the answer in your data and shows results you can click straight through to.

Try asking…You'll get
“Who's travelling in the next 3 months?”A list of customers with upcoming departures
“Who's going to Spain?”Everyone with a Spain trip on file
“How much have we booked this year?”A revenue figure with the trips behind it
“Which customers have gone quiet?”Past customers with no recent contact
“Who's recently returned?”Households back from a trip, ready for a welcome-home
“Tell me about Sarah Thompson”A full profile: who they are, value, trips, risks
“Build me a report for this year”Booked revenue, pipeline, new customers, top destinations

Follow-ups and acting on answers

  • Ask a follow-up. Luna remembers the thread: after “who's travelling next month?” you can just ask “and just the VIPs?”. Click New question to start fresh.
  • Act on the answer. When the answer is a list of customers, buttons appear underneath: Email all, Add to journey and Add tag — the same actions as the customer list, one click from the answer.
Good to knowLive AI (briefs, reply drafts, Ask Luna, smart segments) needs an AI key configured on your workspace — your admin can check this in Settings → Integrations. If it's off, the app still works: briefs show a prepared summary, the inbox shows curated drafts, and the customer search falls back to a rules-based parser. Either way, nothing is lost and nothing breaks.
Top tip: Treat Luna like a brilliant new colleague — let it do the first draft and the legwork, and bring your judgement to the final send. That combination is where the time savings come from.

12 Common workflows

The everyday jobs, start to finish. Follow these and most “how do I…?” questions answer themselves.

Handle a new enquiry

  1. Open the Inbox; the new enquiry will be in the Today lane.
  2. Read Luna's read, pick a reply draft, edit if needed and send (or Draft with Luna for fresh options).
  3. Open the customer (mini-card → Open full record) and check the brief.
  4. On the Trips board, move their trip to Quoted once you've priced it.

Prepare a customer who's about to travel

  1. The Pre-departure check-in journey will have created a task — open it from Tasks.
  2. Open the Customer 360; check the Risk card for passport issues and the household graph for allergies.
  3. Use Draft a reply or Schedule call to confirm final details.
  4. Move the trip to Travelling when they depart.

Re-engage customers who've gone quiet

  1. In Customers, search “booked over a year ago, no contact”.
  2. Save segment so it's one click next week.
  3. Either Email all for a personal note, or Add to journey → Re-engage quiet customers to have Luna draft each one.
  4. Review the drafts in Tasks / the journey feed, then send.

Run a group campaign

  1. Build the audience with a smart search (e.g. “families who've been to Greece”).
  2. Tick the rows you want, or act on the whole view.
  3. Add tag to label them for tracking, then Email selected or Draft outreach.
  4. Later, search by that tag to measure who booked.

After a customer returns

  1. The Welcome home journey drafts an email three days after return — find it in the journey feed.
  2. Review, personalise and send; ask how it went.
  3. Add note on their record with anything to remember for next time.

14 FAQ & troubleshooting

The questions people ask first — answered, so you don't have to ask anyone.

My search returned no customers. Why?
Filter chips combine with AND, so each one narrows the result. Remove a chip (click its ×) to widen the search, or click Clear and start again.
The AI brief says it isn't configured, or drafts look generic.
Live AI needs a key on your workspace (check Settings → Integrations, or ask your admin). Without it the app still works and shows prepared content.
Did that email actually send?
No — Luna only ever creates a draft. You review and send it from your mail client. Journey emails sit as drafts marked “awaiting review”.
Why didn't a journey fire for a particular customer?
Three common reasons: they don't currently match the trigger; Luna has already actioned them (it never repeats); or the journey is paused. Paused journeys aren't evaluated.
“Save segment” shows a message about a migration.
Saving segments needs a one-time database table. Ask your admin to run the segments migration; everything else works meanwhile.
I dragged a trip and it jumped back.
The save didn't go through (usually a brief connection blip). The board snaps back so it stays accurate — just make the move again.
Where did my note go?
Notes appear in the customer's timeline immediately, newest at the top, alongside emails and calls.
How do I get my own customers in / remove the demo data?
The demo households are sample data. Your admin can clear them so you can add real customers; ask them to wipe the demo records when you're ready to go live.
What's the difference between Quick find and Ask Luna?
Quick find (⌘/Ctrl+P) jumps you to a record or page by name. Ask Luna (⌘/Ctrl+K) answers questions about your data.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. The layout adapts: the sidebar becomes a menu button (top-left) and the screens stack into a single column.
How do I switch to dark mode?
Use the sun/moon button at the bottom-left of the sidebar. It's remembered on that device.
Can I see this guide inside the app?
Yes — the in-app tour (sidebar → Take a tour) walks through every screen and links you to it.

15 Glossary

Plain-English definitions of the terms you'll see across Luna Work.

HouseholdA customer unit — a family, couple or individual you serve, with one or more contacts.
ContactA person within a household (lead, partner, child, dependant).
Customer 360The single page showing everything about one household.
BriefLuna's short written summary of a customer — who they are and how they book.
SegmentA saved or ad-hoc group of customers defined by a smart search.
Token / chipOne filter in a smart search (e.g. “Greece”, “Family”). Chips combine with AND.
LTVLifetime value — the total a household has spent with you.
PipelineAll your trips arranged by stage, from Enquiry to Returned.
StageWhere a trip is in its lifecycle (see §6).
LaneAn inbox priority bucket: Today, This week or Later.
TriageLuna sorting inbound messages by urgency into lanes.
JourneyAn auto-pilot rule: a trigger (when) plus an action (what Luna does).
TriggerThe condition that makes a journey act (e.g. 10 days before departure).
EligibleA customer who currently matches a journey's trigger.
TaskA piece of follow-up work in your queue, from a journey or created by you.
PredictionA computed signal on the 360: Opportunity, Trip match or Risk.
PreferenceA recorded customer like/dislike (airline, room, dietary) that feeds matches.
ComplianceConsent and document status: GDPR, marketing opt-in, passport validity.
